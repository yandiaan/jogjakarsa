# Jogjakarsa — travel studio

Purpose: help travellers understand, try and trust a personalised Jogja travel planning service. The product currently uses demonstration data. Actual partners, availability, booking and AI integration remain future work.

Identity: Jogjakarsa. Positioning: Perjalanan personal, berakar di Jogja. Tone: attentive, concrete and calm. Never invent reviews, partner logos, customer counts or AI capability claims.

Direction: cinematic editorial travel with product clarity. Charcoal surfaces, warm ivory type, muted brass accent. Cormorant Garamond for expressive headings and Manrope for readable product copy. Existing drone footage and Prambanan photo anchor the place identity. Motion illustrates turning a travel idea into a tangible route.

References examined: https://nalarx.com/ (product demonstration, clear workflow, restrained serif hero); https://21st.dev/community/components/s/animated-hero (animated hero patterns); https://21st.dev/community/components/s/card (media interaction patterns). These are direction references; implementation uses the existing Astro, React and GSAP stack.

Homepage: immediate value proposition and CTA; expanding video; interactive experience selector; workflow demonstration; configurable itinerary and itemised estimate; human review explanation; FAQ; editorial footer.

Interaction: visible keyboard focus, native buttons and disclosures, reduced motion fallbacks, mobile layouts, video play/pause. Navigation and planner remain directly accessible.

Previous weaknesses: cinematic introduction delayed explaining the service; inconsistent inherited colors; quote included an activity absent from the itinerary; save confirmation had no persistence; scope and FAQs were missing.

## Product clarity across every route

International extension: manual ID/EN copy shares the homepage and planning components, and English culture pages offer visitor overviews linked to the Indonesian archive. Language, display currency and ticket category are separate controls. USD values are approximate conversions of canonical IDR budgets at an explicitly labelled manual demo rate. English-speaking guide, driver-language request and YIA transfer are selectable service requests, not confirmed partners. Native selects and checkboxes make these options usable on phones and with a keyboard. Header spacing collapses to the menu before translated navigation can overflow.

Jogjakarsa is a travel agency being built for custom trips within DIY, initially couples, families and small friend groups. The deliverables are itinerary, local activities, vehicle with driver and itemized costs. The current product is a working planning demo; booking, partner confirmation and human operations are planned. AI is an optional planning aid, not the product being sold.

Nalarx (https://nalarx.com/) informs category-first explanation and concrete workflows. kimkim (https://www.kimkim.com/) informs custom-trip deliverables and the human planning role. Klook (https://www.klook.com/) informs concrete service names. These are messaging references, not evidence of our traction.

The homepage names the offer first, then deliverables, trip options, workflow and demo. Culture pages share the same header, footer, typography and palette, and link knowledge to the planning product. Gold highlights actions and culture context; charcoal retains the approved cinematic photography direction. Asymmetric service rows explain scope without another card grid. Cormorant links the travel and culture pages; Manrope keeps functional copy readable. Existing reveals and photography motion guide reading; reduced motion skips them, and mobile article galleries scroll naturally. Dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.
